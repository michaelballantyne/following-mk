# Fair cross-arm work metric: unifier-level counters

Resolves the "fair work metric" backlog item. Implementation delegated
to a subagent, reviewed and committed by the main session.

## What was inadequate

`*==-counter*` only counts calls through the user-facing `==` wrapper;
mk.scm internals (recursive descent inside `unify`, constraint
reconsideration via `unify*`) bypass it. And `*==/d-counter*` counts a
different call site, so "main work" and "follower work" weren't
comparable, and neither was complete.

## The new counters

- **`unify (main)` / `unify (follower)`** — ticked at the entry of
  mk.scm's `unify`, including recursive calls, so the count scales with
  structure traversed. A boolean global `*in-follower-eval?*` (set by
  `run-and-set-follower` around the follower goal invocation, plain
  set!/restore) routes the tick. Since `unify*` just maps `unify`,
  constraint-reconsideration work is captured too. NOT captured: `walk`
  and `occurs-check` traversals. Committed follower extensions that the
  main search later touches count as main — correct attribution.
- **`conde (main)`** — one tick per main-search conde entry, in
  `main-conde-hook`.
- **`conde/d entries`** — one tick per `conde/d-runtime` evaluation
  attempt inside the follower.

Counters live in mk/mk.scm (the unify pair + flag; a 13-line patch) and
following.scm (the conde pair, registration, flag management).

Sanity checks passed: follower-unify is 0 in no-follower runs;
unify(main) > ==-counter (130k vs 51k on rember-2 baseline); all 34
tests pass.

## Baseline numbers restated in the new metric

**rember-2** (`./run.sh synthesis/rember-2.scm`):

| metric | no follower | with follower | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| unify (main) | 130,621 | 23,391 | 5.6× |
| conde (main) | 13,950 | 2,244 | 6.2× |
| unify (follower) | 0 | 1,611,778 | — |

**ex3** (`--check-follower-every 20`):

| metric | no follower | with follower | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| unify (main) | 1,705,841 | 283,917 | 6.0× |
| conde (main) | 71,196 | 14,221 | 5.0× |
| unify (follower) | 0 | 10,402,194 | — |

## What this says

The follower's main-search savings are a consistent **~5–6× on the
complete metric**, on both benchmarks, whether measured in unifications
or conde expansions. The old `==`-wrapper numbers (~4–5×) were not far
off, but now the claim is principled. Two implications:

1. Under fair interleaving, the savings plateau around 5–6× — real,
   but an order of magnitude short of the research goal. This is the
   number the size-bounded-search experiment has to beat.
2. The follower does ~12–37× more unification work internally than the
   *entire baseline main search* (1.6M vs 131k on rember-2; 10.4M vs
   1.7M on ex3). We ignore overhead for now, but any future overhead
   phase starts from a very deep hole.
